# CRM Glossary

- CRM: Customer Relationship Management, a system for managing customer data and sales interactions.
- Client: A person or company contact stored in the CRM.
- Pipeline: The flow of potential deals through statuses such as new, contacted, qualified, won, and lost.
- Deal value: The estimated money value of a sales opportunity.
- Lead: A potential customer before the sale is confirmed.
- Auth guard: A check that prevents users from opening protected pages without logging in.
- Local storage: Browser storage that keeps data after refresh and browser restart.
- Session storage: Browser storage that is cleared when the tab is closed.
- CRUD: Create, Read, Update, Delete.
- Fetch: A browser API for making network requests.
